Founded 1997

Headquartered at: Joy-Nostalg Center, 17 ADB Avenue, Ortigas Center, Pasig City, Philippines

Asia United Bank Corporation (AUB) is a universal bank in the Philippines that provides a wide range of banking and financial services. Established in 1997, AUB has rapidly grown to become one of the fastest-growing banks in the country, offering innovative products and excellent customer service.

As the banking arm of the Rebisco Group of Companies, AUB combines financial strength with a customer-centric approach to meet the evolving needs of individuals, businesses, and institutions.

Background & History

View Official Company Background
1997

Foundation

Asia United Bank Corporation (AUB) was granted a full-branch commercial bank license and was registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) on October 3, 1997.

AUB commenced operations on October 31, 1997 under the Monetary Board (MB) Resolution No. 1149 dated September 3, 1997.

2012

Expansion

AUB obtained approval from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) to upgrade its license into expanded commercial banking status.

2013

Universal Banking

The Bank was granted authority to operate as a universal bank under MB Resolution No. 356 dated February 28, 2013.

AUB joined the league of Philippine Banks that have become publicly listed.

Present

Continued Growth

Today, AUB is a publicly listed company with three principal/substantial stockholders: Republic Biscuit Corporation, Kuo Yu Philippines Holdings Corporation, and Lambda Holdings Corporation.

AUB continues to grow by building a strong and loyal client base through its careful and focused client generation activities and technological innovations.

Our Foundation

AUB began as a joint venture between a consortium of Filipino Industrialists (namely, Jacinto L. Ng, Sr., Luis U. Ang, and Jose S. Sandejas) and Taiwanese investment banks (namely, China United Trust & Investment Corporation, and China Development Industrial Bank).

Scope of Business/Operation

As a universal bank, AUB is authorized to exercise the following:

  1. Powers of an investment house, including securities underwriting and trading, loan syndication, financial advisory, private placement of debt and equity securities, project finance and direct equity investment.
  2. Power to invest in allied and non-allied enterprises, subject to regulatory caps on the amount of investment relative to the Bank's capital and ownership percentage.
  3. Commercial banking operations including deposit-taking, lending, trade finance, and foreign exchange.
  4. Digital banking services and technological innovations for both deposit-taking and lending businesses.
  5. Trust, investment management, and fiduciary services through its trust products.

Capital Structure

View Capital Structure Information
  • Capital Adequacy Ratio: AUB maintains a strong capital position with a CAR of 17.5%, well above the regulatory minimum of 10%.
  • Ownership Structure: The bank has a diverse ownership base with institutional investors holding 65%, founding family members 25%, and public shareholders 10%.
  • Regulatory Compliance: AUB adheres to Basel III requirements and maintains appropriate capital buffers for risk management.
  • Dividend Policy: The bank has a consistent dividend payout ratio of 20-30% of net income, balancing shareholder returns with capital retention for growth.

Corporate Governance

View Corporate Governance Statement
  • Board Structure: AUB's Board consists of 11 directors, including 4 independent directors, ensuring diverse perspectives and objective governance.
  • Committees: The Board operates through specialized committees including Audit, Risk Management, Corporate Governance, and Executive committees.
  • Transparency: AUB adheres to strict disclosure policies and regularly publishes comprehensive corporate governance reports.
  • Compliance: The bank maintains full compliance with BSP, SEC, and PSE regulations regarding corporate governance practices.
  • Ethics Code: A comprehensive Code of Ethics guides behavior of all employees and executives, with strict enforcement mechanisms.

Corporate Social Responsibility
